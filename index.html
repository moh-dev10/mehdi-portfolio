04 — Project Details

Deep dive: the real estate price model.

How a raw scrape of messy classified ads became a model with an R² of 0.89 and an interactive demo.

Featured case study

Algerian Real Estate Price Predictor

Property listings in Algeria are inconsistent, unstandardised and full of noise. This project builds a reproducible pipeline that cleans, engineers and models them into a trustworthy price estimate.

0R² score
0kListings cleaned
−24%MAE vs baseline
12Engineered features

Real estate listings across Algiers, Oran and Constantine are published in wildly different formats, with prices sometimes per square metre and sometimes per unit, and with location described only as free text.

  • Inconsistent data. Same property described five different ways across platforms.
  • No transparency. Buyers and small agencies have no reference for a fair price.
  • Strong location effect. Commune and distance to centre drive most of the variance.

A fully reproducible pipeline built with Pandas and Scikit-learn, versioned from raw scrape to final model.

  • Cleaning. De-duplicated listings, normalised units and parsed free-text locations.
  • Feature engineering. Surface, rooms, floor, commune, distance to city centre, building age and listing freshness — 12 features total.
  • Modelling. Benchmarked 5 algorithms with 5-fold cross-validation, then tuned Gradient Boosting on RMSE.
  • Explanation. Permutation importance to verify that the model learned sensible drivers.

The tuned Gradient Boosting model beat the linear baseline decisively and shipped as a demo interface for non-technical users.

  • +28% R² compared to the ordinary least-squares baseline.
  • Top predictors: surface area, commune, and distance to the city centre.
  • Error reduced to a median absolute error of roughly 11% of listing price.
  • Shipped a Streamlit demo where a user enters three values and gets an instant estimate.
